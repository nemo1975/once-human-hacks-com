Every hacks seller claims updates, privacy, and instant delivery. For SAND, you should optimize for **honest module lists**, **live status**, and **portal delivery**—not adjectives.

This checklist applies whether you land on sandhacks.com or elsewhere. We are biased toward our own hub, but the steps are game-specific.

## 1. Module list matches Sophie gameplay

A credible Sand Hacks page mentions merchant groups you will actually toggle:

- Aimbot controls (FOV, smoothing, visibility, team filter)
- Player and NPC ESP
- Trampler tracking and loot visualization
- Item ESP including keys and valuables
- Radar with range and size controls
- Object ESP for containers, Dreadnaught, captain quarters, Aurogen
- Misc: compass, panic key, config save/load

Red flags:

- Teleport, noclip, or speed claims not on checkout
- “Undetected forever” headlines
- Skin changers advertised without merchant listing

## 2. Live status with offline language

Good sites say when **not** to play. Bad sites only shout online.

Check:

- Homepage banner
- Discord #status
- Patch-day forum posts

If a seller hides downtime, assume they also hide broken builds.

## 3. Portal delivery, not Telegram zips

After checkout you should get:

- Operator portal login
- Loader tied to your key
- HWID reset flow
- Config backup

Never run binaries from comment sections, YouTube descriptions, or unsolicited DMs.

## 4. Support with reproducible tickets

Email plus Discord ticket channels beat “PM admin.” Good support asks for Windows version, order email, and loader error text—not just “try again.”

See our [support hub](/support/) expectations.

## Interview questions for any seller

Ask (or search their site for answers):

1. Where is offline status posted?
2. What happens to my key during downtime?
3. Which Trampler modules are listed this week?
4. Who answers tickets and in what hours?
5. Can I export configs without cloud?

If question three gets a vague answer, they are reskinning a generic extraction cheat—not maintaining SAND modules.
