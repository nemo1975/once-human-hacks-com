Once Human Hacks troubleshooting is almost always one of five things: status, launch order, Windows, a stale loader, or overlays you did not test.

Work the list in order. Skipping to "reinstall Windows" wastes a night.

## Status first

If the banner is offline, updating, or testing, stop. A Once Human patch can kill the menu bind while the old loader still says compatible. Wait for an online note, then download the portal build.

## Waiting for game window

Switch Once Human to borderless windowed. Close overlay apps and RGB software. Run the loader as administrator. Start it after the main menu, not at the splash. Reboot once if you just added a Defender exclusion.

Fullscreen exclusive is the usual blocker. Forum setup threads linked from Complete repeat this because it keeps working.

## Menu key dead in-game

Grab the current portal file even if yesterday's loader launches. Rebind only after the new build is in place. If status flipped to updating while you were in a session, wait it out. Do not spam inject.

## Defender and SmartScreen

Exclude the portal download folder, not the game install and not your whole user directory. Re-download from the portal. Files from Discord comments are not a support channel.

## Overlay in OBS or Discord

Stream-safe modes are build-specific. Record 20 seconds locally before you go live. After a patch, test again. If it leaks, disable ESP for that session. Capture notes live in the forum, not in a static FAQ.

## Still stuck

Email or Discord with order email, Windows 10 or 11, how you launch Once Human, exact loader text, and a screenshot. The [support page](/support/) checklist exists so tickets are not bounced. Refunds follow [Terms](/terms/). Change of mind and bans are not covered.
