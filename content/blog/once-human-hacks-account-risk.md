## What "undetected" actually means here

It means the current build was online the last time staff checked. It does not mean NetEase ACE, player reports, or a future patch will ignore you. Shops that stamp UNDETECTED in green forever are selling a feeling.

Our homepage banner and Discord #status are the check you run after a client update. If either shows offline or updating, do not queue.

## Reports still happen

Most clips that get people reported are obvious snaps, neon boxes, or ESP that paints the whole map. Conservative FOV, short ESP range, and a panic key do not make you safe. They just remove the loudest reasons a stranger hits report.

ESP-only is still a modification. Creature radar in a PvP compound can look like wallhacks because that is what it is: information through cover.
