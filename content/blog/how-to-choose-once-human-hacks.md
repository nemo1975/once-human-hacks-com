How to choose Once Human Hacks is mostly about ignoring the loudest landing pages. Every competitor promises aimbot, ESP, and "undetected." Very few show a module list you can tick against checkout.

Use this checklist. If a shop fails two or more items, leave.

## 1. Named modules, not a vibe

You want aimbot options you can name: hotkey, FOV, bones, smoothing, visibility, distance. You want ESP groups: players, creatures, items, objects, radar. A single "wallhack" bullet is not a list.

Our [Complete page](/products/once-human-hacks/complete/) is built that way on purpose. Compare it to the seller page before you pay.

## 2. Status you can see

A timestamped online/offline note beats a green badge from last season. After a Once Human patch, the honest move is "wait." Shops that tell you to queue on an old build are not on your side.

## 3. Portal delivery

Keys and loaders should unlock in an account portal, not a zip in a ticket. Updates during the license window should be listed. If setup only exists as a 40-message Discord scroll, budget extra time.

## 4. Support that asks for details

Good support asks for order email, Windows version, and exact loader text. Bad support sends a new file in DMs. Our [support page](/support/) lists the ticket checklist.

## 5. Claims to skip

Permanent undetected. Zero ban risk. Guaranteed ACE bypass. "Works on every launcher forever." Ban recovery via spoofer. If you see those, read the [account risk](/blog/once-human-hacks-account-risk/) article and walk away.

## 6. Utilities stay utilities

Cloud DMA, HWID Spoofer, and Skin Changer are separate checkouts here. A shop that hides required extras until after payment is not a bargain.
