Once Human aimbot settings are where most first-time buyers either look natural or look like a highlight reel. The merchant list is not mysterious: hotkey, FOV, bones, smoothing, prediction, visibility, team filters, and a max distance cap. The work is picking values you can live with for an hour, not chasing a perfect slider.

This guide is for the Complete suite on Windows PC. Confirm every toggle on checkout. We do not invent extra aim modes.

## Start with FOV you can see

Enable the FOV circle so you know when assist is even considering a target. A wide circle will pull across half the screen. That feels strong in empty terrain and looks awful in a settlement clip.

Keep the first session modest. If you miss close fights, raise FOV a little. If the reticle jumps to people you were not aiming at, shrink it. Dynamic FOV, when listed, should stay off until the base circle feels stable.

## Smoothing before bone tricks

Smoothing is the difference between a pull and a snap. Higher smoothing slows the reticle. Lower smoothing slams it. Start higher than you think you need, then drop it until tracking keeps up with rifles at mid range.

Humanized trajectories, when listed, sit on top of smoothing. They do not replace a bad FOV. If the reticle still slams, smoothness is the first knob, not bone priority.

## Bone selection and visibility

Head priority looks cracked on a standing target in the open. It also flicks when someone peeks a window. Chest plus a visibility check is the usual starting pair for rifles.

Team filtering keeps squadmates out of the pull. Max target distance stops long-range snaps across empty map. Leave both on until you understand how the overlay behaves after a patch.

Prediction helps on strafing targets. It is not a substitute for leading shots yourself on long lanes. If prediction feels late or early after an update, drop it and retest.

## Hotkey hold versus always-on

Hold-to-aim is easier to explain in a clip and easier to turn off when you are looting. Always-on tracking across open terrain is how people get reported. Use the customizable hotkey, keep a panic key in reach, and save the profile before you change six sliders at once.

## Pairing with ESP

Aimbot does not tell you who is behind a wall. Player ESP and radar do. If you bought Complete, test aimbot in a quiet area with ESP range short, then raise ESP only if the overlay stays readable. See the [ESP and radar guide](/blog/once-human-esp-and-radar/) for overlay density.

## After a patch

Once Human updates can change how FOV and smoothing feel even when the menu still opens. Check the status banner, download the portal build, and reload a conservative preset instead of yesterday's maxed sliders. The [setup guide](/blog/once-human-hacks-setup-guide/) covers launch order if the menu bind dies.

Save two configs: a tight PvP preset and a slightly wider open-world preset. Do not share those files in public Discord channels.
