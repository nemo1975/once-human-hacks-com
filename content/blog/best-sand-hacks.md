Open any SAND hacks shop and you will see the same three words: instant, private, updated. None of those words tell you what is inside the download, and that is the part you are paying for on Sophie.

Here is how to judge a package before you commit to an extract run with third-party tools attached.

## Start with what the hack actually does

SAND: Raiders of Sophie is an open-world PvPvE extraction shooter. You cross dunes, loot cities, fight Upiors, dodge other raiders, and time pulls around walking Tramplers and naval threats like the Dreadnaught. Awareness and aim both matter, but the merchant module list is what you should verify—not a marketing blurb.

**Aim assistance.** Merchant-listed aimbot modules include a customizable hotkey, adjustable FOV, bone selection, humanized trajectories, prediction, visibility checks, dynamic FOV, smoothing, team filtering, and maximum target distance. Smoothing is the sleeper setting. A hard snap looks ugly in a clip; a gentler pull is easier to live with near extract elevators.

**Visuals and radar.** Player ESP with distance, health, shield, kit, and held item is the baseline for PvP. NPC ESP helps with Upior patrols. Trampler tracking, loot visualization, and radar indicators matter when you are routing toward a hijack or away from a deck gun. Item ESP for keys, Aurogen crystals, and valuables saves time in captain quarters and buried treasure spots.

**Quality of life.** Compass, custom crosshair, FPS display, menu key, panic key, configurable colors, and config save/load sound minor until you are mid-raid on a contested extract. Our [aimbot settings guide](/blog/sand-aimbot-settings/) walks through each slider.

## Status beats promises

No honest seller can promise a hack stays ignored forever. A SAND patch can change things overnight. What you can demand is a live status on the product page and a seller willing to say, in plain words, that you should re-check after updates. Pages that promise permanent safety are telling you something about themselves.

## What happens after you pay

A good checkout is boring. You pick a plan, pay, and the loader and setup guide unlock in your portal. Updates during your active license window are included. Support answers when something breaks. You also want Windows 10 or 11, Intel or AMD hardware, and an HWID reset path if you change PCs. If you are unsure about the reset, read our [HWID reset walkthrough](/blog/sand-hwid-reset/). ## Sophie-specific modules worth confirming

Before you trust a landing page, open checkout and confirm these groups still match the merchant list:

**Trampler.** Player and NPC Trampler tracking, loot visualization, and Trampler radar are the difference between guessing which deck was looted and routing with intent. If a seller only shows generic player boxes, you are not getting a Sophie-aware package.

**Object ESP.** Containers, alarm boxes, Aurogen crystals, buried treasure, auto turrets, Dreadnaught markers, captain quarters, and corpse loot are how you plan naval and city arcs without wandering. Cross-check spelling against checkout—renamed POIs happen after content updates.

**Radar and compass.** Smooth radar rotation plus adjustable range keeps your minimap honest when vertical Tramplers break line of sight. Compass and smoothed heading belong in misc but matter on dunes where audio falls off.

## Mistakes first-time buyers make

Buying aim-only when you solo queue and never hear footsteps two alleys over. Running max ESP distance in downtown and blaming FPS. Ignoring offline status because the game still launches vanilla. Sharing portal logins with a duo partner instead of buying a second key. Importing config files from unknown forums on patch week.

Each of those is fixable; two of them can end an account.
