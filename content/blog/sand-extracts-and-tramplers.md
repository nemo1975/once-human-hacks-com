Extracts in SAND: Raiders of Sophie are timers plus geometry. Tramplers add a moving skyline that blocks sightlines and attracts third parties. Sand Hacks ESP and radar exist to make those problems legible—not to autopilot you to the evac ship.

## City extracts: verticality and camps

Urban blocks reward:

- Short **max ESP distance**
- **Corner boxes** instead of full skeleton
- **Radar** with small range so dots match audio cues
- **Item ESP** for keys when contracts demand them

ESP shines when someone holds parallel stair or elevator angles you cannot hear. It does not replace listening for Trampler footsteps one block over.

Forum: [city extract ESP thread](/forum/trampler-extract-esp-in-cities/).

## Open dunes: distance and direction

On dunes you trade building cover for line of sight. Raise ESP distance modestly, enable **direction indicators**, and keep **compass** visible if you run Complete misc.

Trampler silhouettes can mislead from far away—use Trampler radar to separate player-controlled decks from ambient convoys when merchant modules allow.

## Trampler hijack routes

Typical hijack flow:

1. Spot Trampler with radar or audio.
2. Enable **Trampler loot visualization** when you commit to boarding.
3. Route with **object ESP** for ladders and deck turrets
4. Disable noisy overlays before boarding if FPS tanks

Deck fights are close quarters. Swap to [deck preset](/blog/sand-hacks-config-presets/) with hold aim and tiny FOV if you use aimbot at all.

## Upiors and NPC ESP

NPC ESP helps tag undead patrol pressure so you do not waste magazines before a player third-parties you. Filter snaplines—Upiors in alleys already clutter the screen.

## Loot arcs: captain quarters and Aurogen

Object ESP categories for high-value routes:

- Captain quarters in naval/city arcs
- Aurogen crystals when mining contracts active
- Buried treasure markers in dune events
- Explosive barrels near industrial Trampler yards

Pair item ESP filters so keys and valuables do not paint the entire HUD.

## Dreadnaught pressure

When Dreadnaught zones matter, toggle object ESP for naval mines, projectile shields, and Dreadnaught markers. Radar range medium; audio cues still win for incoming shell warnings.

## Extract timing beats overlay stacking

Overlays can make you slow. Raiders die with full ESP because they stared at boxes instead of the timer. Practice with:

- Panic key muscle memory
- Minimal presets for final minute pushes
- Squad roles: one ESP-heavy caller, one lighter HUD

## Aimbot during extract fights

If you run aim modules, keep **visibility check** on near elevator camps. Smoothing high, FOV small. Winning the fight still means nothing if the timer expires—see [aimbot settings](/blog/sand-aimbot-settings/).

## Contract-type routing

**Key contracts:** filter item ESP to keys and containers near known districts; disable unrelated valuables until the key is secure.

**Aurogen runs:** object ESP for crystals plus compass heading on dunes; Trampler radar when cutting through industrial short paths.

**Naval / Dreadnaught arcs:** mines and projectile shield markers save more time than player nicknames—trim player ESP clutter before boarding zones.

## Third-party timing

Extract beacons draw rats. ESP helps you see them early; it does not stop them from waiting one floor down. Hold grenades and utility in reserve even when boxes say the route is clear—merchant ESP cannot read patience.

## Solo vs trio pacing

Solos should run lean HUD. Trios can afford one player with wide item ESP while another runs combat aim preset on Complete. Voice comms beat duplicate radar beeps.
