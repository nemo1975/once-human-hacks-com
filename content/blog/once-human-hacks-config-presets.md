Once Human Hacks config presets exist so you stop rebuilding FOV and ESP range every time you log in. The misc panel lists configuration save and load. Use it.

## PvP preset

Tight aimbot FOV. Higher smoothness. Chest bone plus visibility check. Team filter on. Short player ESP distance. Corner boxes, names off, skeleton only in interiors. Creature radar dim or off in compounds. Item ESP limited to keys and valuables.

This is the profile you load before a contested fight. It will feel weak in empty terrain. That is the point.

## Loot and open-world preset

Slightly wider FOV if you still want assist. Longer ESP and radar range. Creature tracking on. Item and object ESP for containers, medical loot, and resources. Keep colors dim. A huge radar is still a bad idea.

Use this when you are farming, not when you are holding a doorway.

## Optional creature-hunt preset

Creature radar and loot visualization up. Player ESP still capped. Aimbot FOV modest. This is for PvE routes where entities matter more than other Metas.

## How to save without drama

Change one group at a time. Save after the overlay stays readable for ten minutes. Name files like `pvp-settlement` and `open-loot`, not `final-final2`. After a patch, reload the conservative PvP file first. Do not import a stranger's JSON because it has "god" in the filename.

Cloud configs follow the license after an HWID reset for some users. Export a local copy anyway. Details are in the [HWID reset](/blog/once-human-hwid-reset/) guide.

If the menu will not open after a client update, skip presets until the portal build is current. The [setup guide](/blog/once-human-hacks-setup-guide/) covers launch order.
